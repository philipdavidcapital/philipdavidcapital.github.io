Privacy

Scope

This Privacy Notice applies to personal information collected by Philip David Capital Management LLC ("Philip David Capital Management," "we," "our," or "us") through this website. It describes the types of personal information we collect, how we use it, with whom we share it, and the rights and choices available to individuals regarding such information. This notice does not apply to personal information collected through other channels or to personal information of investors, which is governed by separate privacy notices provided to investors.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including any contact details and the content of communications you submit through this website or by email. We also collect technical information automatically when you visit, including internet protocol address, browser type, operating system, referring website, pages viewed, and dates and times of access. This information is collected through standard web server logs and similar technologies, and is used to operate, secure, and improve the website.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to respond to inquiries and communications, to administer and develop our relationship with you and any organization you represent, to operate, secure, and improve the website, to comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations, and for other legitimate business purposes consistent with this notice.

Disclosure of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose personal information to service providers acting on our behalf and bound by confidentiality obligations, to professional advisers including legal, tax, and accounting advisers, to regulators and other authorities where required by law or legal process, and to successors in interest in connection with any reorganization or transfer of business. Where information is transferred outside your home jurisdiction, we apply appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to support technical functioning of the site and to gather aggregate usage information. Most web browsers allow you to manage cookie preferences through browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect the functioning of certain features of the website.

Data Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. No system of electronic transmission or storage is, however, completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of any information transmitted to or stored on the website.

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights with respect to personal information we hold about you, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our processing, and to data portability where applicable. To exercise any such rights, or to ask any question regarding this notice, please contact us using the contact details below.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for adults and is not directed at children under the age of eighteen. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we may take appropriate action.

Changes to this Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. The most current version will be posted on this website. We encourage you to review this notice periodically for any changes.

Contacting Us

For questions regarding this Privacy Notice or our handling of personal information, please contact us at clientservices@philipdavidcapital.com or by mail at Philip David Capital Management LLC, 4815 East 112th Street, Tulsa, Oklahoma 74137.
